## Summary

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.5546054.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5546054)

The [Research Software Community Database]({{ page.github }}) dataset is a repository of research software, generated using the [rse software](https://rseng.github.io/rse/) that has scrapers to automatically update from peer-reviewed or respectable sources. An example analysis has been [done](https://github.com/rseng/rsepedia-analysis) using a [taxonomy and criteria](https://github.com/rseng/rseng) for research software, and [published](https://openresearchsoftware.metajnl.com/articles/10.5334/jors.359).
