## Summary

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/805254424.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20112395)

The [JobSpec Database]({{ page.github }}) is a repository of over 33K job specifications across 6 workload managers that were searched for and saved from GitHub. 

## Dataset

The data files are organized by repository in `data`. A chromedriver is needed for generation, and full instructions are provided in the repository. Additional data files are extracted, and scripts included to count directives, and calculate cyclomatic complexity.
